What the app does
  1. Scores every player for every role in your formation (key ×5, preferred ×3, useful ×1), weighted by how natural the slot is for them.
  2. Picks the best XI with an optimal assignment (no greedy blocking), then finds weak slots and thin cover.
  3. Ranks transfer targets by upgrade margin per slot, showing score ranges for partially-scouted players.
  4. Builds a value-for-money transfer plan within your budget, with plain-English reasons, and lets you compare Plan A vs Plan B.
