How to Read This
  • Sell — surplus to requirements. High sell priority = sell first (usually high value + declining or stagnant).
  • Loan — young player who needs game time to develop. Not ready for first team but has potential.
  • Promote — youth player already outperforming the senior starter. Give them first-team minutes.
  • Keep — Key — current starter. Untouchable.
  • Keep — Backup — within 2 points of starter. Useful squad depth.
  • Keep — Prospect — young player with good potential. Future asset.
  • Keep — Monitor — borderline case. Watch development before deciding.
  • Sell Priority — higher = sell sooner. Factors in surplus gap, age, transfer value, role congestion, and personality.
  • Rank at Role — depth chart position (1 = starter, 2 = first backup, etc.).
